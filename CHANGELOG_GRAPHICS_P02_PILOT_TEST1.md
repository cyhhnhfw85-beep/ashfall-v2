# ASHFALL 0.9.3 — GRAPHICS P02 PILOT TEST1

Source : ASHFALL 0.9.3 GFX2 Pilote 1×2 TEST1.

- Pilot room only: large industrial independent material panels, varied metal/concrete and subtle wear.
- New 2× raster assets in `assets/p02/` for 6 distinct plates and modular wall section.
- Architectural amber lighting, restrained cyan door framing, unchanged door positions and states.
- Solid obstacle images retained; contact shadows improved and visual overhang reduced (1.16×).
- No gameplay, geometry, generator, camera, HUD, enemy, extraction or save data changes.
- Other rooms retain old Periphery rendering; historic rooms are not redesigned.

Known limitations: no full automated real-browser expedition test or iPhone FPS measurement; sprites for obstacles/doors still reuse P01 imagery; the result is a first artistic integration, not a full reskin.
